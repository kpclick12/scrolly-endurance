# Anatomibilder, sektion 04

Genererade 6 september 2026 med inbyggd bildgenerering. Originalen sparas i projektet, inga externa bildanrop behövs. Bilderna är pedagogiska illustrationer och inte ett porträtt av Almgren. Texterna, pilarna, utsnittet och diagrammen byggs i HTML/SVG.

## Löparen

Fil: `assets/runner-anatomy.png`

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: anatomical running illustration for a Swedish editorial scrollytelling, to replace stick figures. Create a single full-body distance runner in strict side profile facing RIGHT, in late stance with rear leg extending left behind, front knee lifted forward right; lean athletic adult male with realistic human proportions, not a bodybuilder. Refined vintage sports physiology atlas: precise graphite contour and fine engraved hatching with restrained watercolor, warm ivory background #fffdf7, muted terracotta muscle fibers, dark forest green plain running shorts, neutral offwhite shoes. Head and torso skin in pale graphite wash; exposed anatomical muscle illustration on both legs (quadriceps, hamstrings, gastrocnemius and clearly defined pale Achilles tendon down to heel), clean educational illustration, no blood or gore. Full figure occupies 85% image height, entire feet and head inside frame, generous plain margins, centered, portrait 2:3 composition. Soft dimensional anatomical modelling and exquisite fine pen work, scientifically plausible joints. NO text, NO labels, NO arrows, NO frames, NO extra diagrams, NO scenery, NO logos, NO decorative splashes. This is a generic illustrated runner, not a portrait of a named athlete.

## Syrets väg

Fil: `assets/oxygen-organs.png`

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: three anatomical illustrations on a single landscape sheet for an editorial physiology explainer. Plain uniform warm ivory #fffdf7 background. Three separated subjects in equal thirds LEFT TO RIGHT, centered on same horizontal axis, with generous blank gutters: LEFT a pair of human lungs front view with trachea and branching bronchial tree, muted slate blue washes and delicate graphite hatching; CENTER an anatomically recognizable human heart external anterior view with aortic arch, pulmonary trunk and vena cava emerging correctly, muted terracotta red and slate blue great vessels, visible coronary arteries, realistic conical organic shape NOT a heart symbol; RIGHT a fusiform skeletal muscle bundle, terracotta parallel muscle fibers, pale tendons at top and bottom, small red and blue capillary branches around the fibers. Refined medical atlas engraved pen and watercolor, substantial fine anatomical detail but clearly legible silhouette, dimensional form. Entire objects inside their own column, equal visual weight. No text, no labels, no arrows, no frames, no inset, no numbers, no background texture, no shadows, no gore, no decoration, no watermark. Landscape 3:2 composition, illustration objects occupy middle 70 percent of image height.
