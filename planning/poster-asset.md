## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original landscape artwork for a Swedish marathon editorial website cover, no typography in bitmap. Scene: a stream of runners seen mostly from behind crossing a New York suspension bridge toward the distance; strong diagonal bridge deck recedes from lower right toward upper left, suspension cables create rhythmic verticals, a distant Manhattan skyline across cool blue water. Original composition, not a recreation of any existing marathon poster. Style: collectible 1980s sporting-event fine-art poster, hand-pulled silkscreen and loose gouache, confident irregular ink shapes, lively human silhouettes with plausible running anatomy, subtle ink registration and paper grain, not smooth vector or photorealism. Palette: predominantly deep forest green, chalk white and dusty cornflower blue, small vivid coral red and golden yellow touches in runners' shirts and sunlight. Restrained overall, no rainbow, no beige or yellowed paper, no decorative splatters. Composition: wide 3:2 landscape, full-bleed scene without frame, runners large enough to read, compelling sense of shared forward motion and city scale. No lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures or recognizable athletes. This will sit below a large black serif headline on pure white webpage.
